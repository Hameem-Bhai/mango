✨ Complete Feature Specification

Every capability engineered and implemented specifically for Mr. Mango BD:

🛍️

Storefront & Shopping Experience

  • Header & Shop Dropdown: Full access to all product categories (Disposables, E-Liquids, Pod Kits, Coils & Cartridges, All Products).
  • Live Instant Search: Debounced instant search bar across names, brands, and flavors.
  • Category Filtering & Sorting: Filter by category, sort by price (low to high, high to low), rating, and featured.
  • Urgency Stock Badges: "Only a few left!" and "Sold Out" tags to boost conversions.
  • New Arrival Badges: Green highlighted flags on latest additions.
💬

WhatsApp Instant Sales Automation

  • Floating WhatsApp Support: Quick-action button linking directly to the Kuril outlet hotline (+880 1700-000000).
  • Click-to-Order via WhatsApp: Customers can order any product directly on WhatsApp with pre-filled product name and price.
  • Instant Order Dispatch: After placing an order, one-tap notification sends complete order details to staff via WhatsApp.
  • Out-of-Stock Waitlist: Customers can submit their WhatsApp number to get notified when restocked.
💳

Bangladeshi Payment System

  • bKash Direct Payment: Dedicated pink bKash card with personal Send Money instructions and order number reference.
  • Nagad Direct Payment: Orange Nagad card with full transaction reference workflow.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Same-day delivery inside Dhaka and courier nationwide.
  • SSLCommerz API Scaffold: Backend architecture ready for automated debit/credit card & MFS processing.
  • Promo / Discount Coupons: Real-time coupon code validation (e.g., MANGO10 for 10% off).
📦

Live Order Tracking & Delivery

  • Live Tracking Engine: Interactive timeline tracking 4 phases (Order Placed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered).
  • Auto-Prefilled Confirmation: Clicking "Track My Order" automatically fills the tracking form with the order number.
  • Delivery Badging: Dhaka same-day delivery badges & 64-district nationwide courier integration info.
  • Store Pickup Option: Option for free in-store pickup at Kuril flagship outlet.
🔒

Secret Staff & Admin Dashboard

  • Invisible to Normal Users: Zero public admin links or staff URLs exposed in navigation or footers.
  • Secure Dual Authentication: Instant admin redirection when registering or signing in with mango@gmail.com / mangokhabo.
  • Product Management: Real-time product creation, price editing, stock toggle, and featured badge switches.
  • Order Lifecycle Manager: Update order status (Pending → Processing → Shipped → Delivered) in one click.
  • Waitlist & Contact Leads: Live inbox of customer waitlist inquiries and wholesale leads.
📍

Physical Outlet Integration

  • Accurate Flagship Store: 2nd floor, Tong Market, Kuril, Beside Main gate of AIUB, Dhaka.
  • Outlet Showcase Card: Features, opening hours (11:00 AM – 11:00 PM), in-store device testing info.
  • Interactive Directions: One-click navigation and direct store helpline connection.
  • Multi-Channel Presence: Outlet details embedded in Hero, Navbar drawer, Footer, Contact, and About pages.
🌓

Dark Mode & Premium UX

  • Dark / Light Mode Toggle: Instant switch with local storage persistence and system preference detection.
  • Mobile Bottom Navigation: Native app-style fixed bottom navigation bar on smartphones (Home, Shop, Cart, Contact).
  • Product Comparison Tool: Compare up to 3 products side-by-side (puffs, nicotine, price, specs).
  • Recently Viewed Carousel: Auto-saves customer's recently explored items for higher return purchases.

Compliance, SEO & Performance

  • Age Verification Gate (18+ / 21+): Legal compliance overlay on first visit with session memory.
